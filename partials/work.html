Echo Bay

Mobile & Web App · Local-First PWA · Flutter

Echo Bay is a fun side project. It started as the usual social app that mixes features from the big ones, but the UI looked boring. Then I watched a playthrough of one of my favourite story games, and the look came from there, even the wording: you "sign the cover" to sign up and "open your sketchbook" to sign in. It's built in Flutter with Drift (SQLite), so it works offline and the data stays on the device. Next is real end-to-end encryption (the Vault schema already has a spare ciphertext column) plus a server to sync across devices.

Flutter, Dart, Drift (SQLite), PWA, Netlify
